# Privacy Policy: AlphaSheetStudio Pin Publisher

**Operator and business name:** AlphaSheetStudio, also known as Practical Spreadsheets (shop: https://www.etsy.com/shop/AlphaSheetStudio)

Last updated: 2026-10-23

AlphaSheetStudio Pin Publisher is a single-user application operated by AlphaSheetStudio, the owner of the AlphaSheetStudio shop. It publishes Pins to AlphaSheetStudio's own Pinterest account.

- What it accesses: AlphaSheetStudio's own Pinterest boards and Pins, through Pinterest's official API and OAuth, with the scopes boards:read, pins:read and pins:write.
- What it stores: AlphaSheetStudio's OAuth token, on the operator's own computer only. Nothing is uploaded to any other service.
- Other users: the tool does not collect, store, sell or share any information about other Pinterest users or visitors.
- No scraping, no credentials: the tool never asks for or stores a Pinterest password and does not use session cookies.
- Removal: access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest account settings (Apps).
- Contact: via the AlphaSheetStudio shop page on Etsy (https://www.etsy.com/shop/AlphaSheetStudio) or Payhip (https://payhip.com/AlphaSheetStudio).
