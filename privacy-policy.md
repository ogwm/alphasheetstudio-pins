# Privacy Policy — AlphaSheetStudio Pin Publisher

Last updated: 2026-10-22

This tool is a single-user application operated by the owner of the AlphaSheetStudio shop. It publishes Pins to the owner's own Pinterest account.

- What it accesses: the owner's own Pinterest boards and Pins, through Pinterest's official API and OAuth, with the scopes boards:read, pins:read and pins:write.
- What it stores: the owner's OAuth token, on the owner's own computer only. Nothing is uploaded to any other service.
- Other users: the tool does not collect, store, sell or share any information about other Pinterest users or visitors.
- No scraping, no credentials: the tool never asks for or stores a Pinterest password and does not use session cookies.
- Removal: the owner can revoke access at any time in Pinterest account settings (Apps).
- Contact: via the AlphaSheetStudio shop page on Etsy or Payhip.
